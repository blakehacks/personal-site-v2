In 2025 I finished a family history book I'd been working on for five years. Lives and Fates covers my family on both of my parents' sides, starting with my grandparents and going back two more generations. It has family trees, every documentary source I could find, and biographies of more than thirty ancestors — some a few lines long, some eight pages. The finished book is a 140-page hardcover; I printed thirty-five copies for family and friends, and you can read the whole thing below.

Want to make your own? I wrote up exactly how I did it in [How to Write a Family History](/blog/how-to-write-a-family-history/).
